Four ways in, because a wheel is not universal

Scroll the wheel, press the plus and minus buttons, use the +, - and 0 keys, or type a level straight into the field. Scrolling alone would leave out anyone on a keyboard or a touch screen.

The range is 100% to 400%. The buttons and keys move 50% a press, the wheel 25% a notch; anything further is quicker typed.

The wheel magnifies towards the pointer, so the detail you are pointing at stays under it. Once magnified, drag with the pointer or use the arrow keys to move around; at rest the arrow keys change picture instead.
