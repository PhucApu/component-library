Five treatments, and one rule behind all of them

Every one answers :focus-within as well as :hover. A treatment that only appears under the pointer tells a keyboard user nothing, and a touch screen has no hovering at all. Tab through these and each lights up in turn.

  • Lift

    Rises, gains a shadow, and warms its border.

  • Zoom

    The picture grows inside a frame that does not, so nothing reflows.

  • Border

    An accent ring and a soft glow, drawn over the card.

  • Spotlight

    Follows the pointer. The one treatment that needs a script, and it only listens while it is the chosen one.

Reveal — a row that arrives with the pointer

The controls slide up over the picture. They are real buttons, so they are in the tab order whether or not they are on show — which is why focus has to bring them out.

  • Reveal

    Tab into the card and the row comes up on its own.

  • None

    No treatment at all, for a card that is content rather than a control.
