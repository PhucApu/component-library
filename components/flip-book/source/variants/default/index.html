Eight pages, four leaves

The book opens closed: the whole stack is on the right, and the edges of the leaves underneath show how much of it there is. Take hold of the top page and pull left — it follows your hand round the spine, and it is a real turn, so letting go halfway drops it back where it was.

Every leaf carries two pages, front and back, exactly as a sheet of paper does. Turning one therefore leaves a page on the left and shows the next on the right, rather than swapping one picture for another.
