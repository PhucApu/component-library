Slide — the track itself moving

The default, and the only one that is really scrolling. It keeps the browser's own swiping and momentum, because nothing has been taken over.

Fade — one picture over another

Fading cannot be done by scrolling: the pictures have to sit on top of one another rather than beside one another. The element stacks them once it loads, which is why every effect still falls back to the same scrolling strip without a script.

Zoom — arriving from slightly too close

Cover — travelling over the one it replaces

The picture on its way out stays put and in view while the new one crosses over it. That needs the two to be told apart, which is the one thing a single CSS rule cannot do on its own.
