Panels that open and close on their own

The widget is details and summary. Turn scripting off and every panel still opens; the component adds the movement, the heading, the region, and the arrow keys between the headers.

What the component adds

A height that moves instead of jumping, a heading for every summary so the panels can be jumped between, a region so each panel can be reached, and ArrowUp and ArrowDown to move between the headers.

What the browser was already doing

Reporting the open state, answering Enter and Space, and keeping the closed content in the document where find-in-page can still reach it.

Why the press is intercepted

The toggle event cannot be cancelled and arrives after the panel has already changed, and details has no beforetoggle. The press is the only thing left that can be refused, so that is where the movement hangs.

Writing the markup

A summary and whatever should be inside. A heading, a panel wrapper and a marker are supplied where they are missing, and anything you write yourself is left alone.
