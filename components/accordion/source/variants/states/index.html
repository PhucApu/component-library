Off, open, and too long for the frame

The unavailable panel still takes focus and still says it is unavailable. Taking it out of the tab order would hide the fact, and a disabled element cannot hold focus at all — turn one off under somebody's finger and the keyboard goes with it.

Open to begin with

The panel carries open in the markup. The component reads that state rather than setting it, so the panel is already open before the script runs and does not animate on arrival.

Not available on this plan

This panel cannot be opened, so nothing here is reachable.

More than fits

A panel taller than the space it has scrolls inside itself rather than pushing the group past the frame it sits in.

The scrolling is a class on the content, not a rule of the component: only the author knows whether a long panel should scroll or make the page longer.

The height the panel opens to is measured from the content, so a scrolling panel stops at its own limit instead of trying to reach the full height of what is inside it.

Nothing about the movement changes. The duration follows the distance travelled, floored and capped so a short panel is not sluggish and a long one does not feel broken.

This last paragraph exists to make sure there is something below the fold to scroll to.
