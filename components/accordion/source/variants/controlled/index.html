Driven from outside the group

expandAll(), collapseAll(), expand() and the expanded property, all reported back through accordion-toggle and accordion-change. The events say what caused the change, so a demo can tell a press apart from a call.

Can a panel be opened from elsewhere on the page?

Yes. The buttons above are ordinary buttons calling ordinary methods; nothing about them is part of the component.

What happens to open-all in exclusive mode?

It opens the first and no more. A group whose rule is one at a time cannot be left in a state that rule forbids, or the next press appears to do nothing.

What level are these headings?

h4, set with heading-level. The right level depends on what is above the group on the page, which the component cannot know.
