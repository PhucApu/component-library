Eight pictures on one ring

The ring drifts a whole turn every thirty seconds. Point at any picture and the ring stops where it stands rather than snapping to the nearest one, so you can look at what is in front of you. Take the pointer away and it carries on from the angle it stopped at.

Take hold of it and pull sideways to bring a picture round to the front, or press the arrow at either side to go one picture at a time. Let go of a drag while still moving and the ring coasts, then goes the rest of the way onto the nearest picture: that one leaves the ring towards you and the others fade back. Only the picture facing you is ever singled out — pointing at one is how you stop the ring, not how you choose it.

Turning the ring yourself, by any of those routes, also stops the drift: motion that carried on would take the picture you just chose straight back off the front.
