Six degrees a second

A full turn every minute. Slow enough that the ring reads as a standing object rather than a moving one, which is what a gallery beside body text wants.

Thirty-six degrees a second

A full turn every ten seconds, which is about as fast as a picture can travel and still be worth pointing at. Speed is capped at 120; past that the ring is a blur with pictures in it.

The other way round

direction="reverse" turns the ring the other way. It changes nothing else: dragging, throwing and the arrow keys all still mean what they meant, and the drift picks up in reverse wherever they leave it.
