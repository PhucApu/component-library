Still until something touches it

Move across the surface and it answers the way water answers a boat: a wake opening behind wherever the pointer has just been, wider the faster it travels. Press anywhere and rings spread from that point and disappear, as though something had fallen in. Leave it alone and it goes flat again — and asks for no frames at all while it is flat.

The heading and the button below sit under the water. The surface takes no presses of its own, so the button still works and the words can still be selected.
