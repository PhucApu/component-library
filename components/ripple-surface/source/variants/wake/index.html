The wake on its own

no-drop leaves only the trail. A mark is made every time the pointer has travelled spacing pixels, not on every move the browser reports: tying it to distance rather than to events is what makes the same gesture look the same on a slow machine and a fast one.

Each mark is an arc facing the way the pointer came from, and it opens wider the faster the pointer was going — a crawl leaves a narrow line, a dash leaves a spreading V.

The same surface with spacing="40" and spacing="6". Wide spacing reads as a heavy hull leaving separate waves; tight spacing reads as a continuous furrow and costs more to draw.
