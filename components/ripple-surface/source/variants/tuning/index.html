How long the water keeps moving

drop-duration and wake-duration are the life of one ripple in milliseconds. Short lives read as shallow water; long ones as a heavy swell. Both are clamped, because a ripple that outlived the visit would still be spreading when the next reader arrived.

How hard it draws

The ink and the strength are the component's own custom properties, so a page sets them the way it sets any other colour. This pair is drawn in the same ink at --ripple-strength: 0.35 and 1: the first is a surface you notice only if you look, the second is one that announces itself.
