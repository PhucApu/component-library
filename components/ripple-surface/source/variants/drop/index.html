Rings on their own

no-wake leaves only what a press makes: three rings following each other out from the point, thinning as they widen and gone by the time they reach the edge. Press in several places and the sets overlap — none of them cancels another, because each ring only knows when it was born.

A tap does the same thing. Dragging a finger does not: on a touch screen that is the page being scrolled, and a surface that answered it would be fighting the gesture it is drawn on top of.

One ring, and six

rings="1" is a single pulse, which suits a press that has to acknowledge something without drawing attention. rings="6" is a heavier object hitting the water.
