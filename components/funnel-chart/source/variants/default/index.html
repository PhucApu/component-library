Where the people went

A funnel is read for two things: what fraction survived end to end, and which step is costing the most. Both are worked out and written above the bars rather than left for the reader to derive.

The shaded run is the loss

It starts exactly where the bar ends and runs back to where the previous bar stopped, so the piece that fell off is drawn at the size it actually was. In most funnels that quantity gets no ink at all — you are left subtracting two numbers to find the thing the chart was drawn to show.

Marked by count, not by rate

The worst rate is usually the last step of a long funnel, where a handful of people are left and losing three of them reads as a catastrophe. The worst count is where the work is. The marking is a word as well as a fill, so it survives a greyscale print.
