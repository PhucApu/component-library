What pinning changes, and what it does not

Pinned, the two funnels share one ruler and mobile draws at the size it actually is. The rates do not move — they never depended on the drawing — which is the useful half of the lesson: a shape can mislead while every number beside it stays true.

Step rates only, here

Both funnels have different tops, so "of the top" means a different denominator in each column and the two are not comparable side by side. The step rate is, which is why it is the one left on.
