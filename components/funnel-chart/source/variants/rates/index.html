Two rates, two different questions

Of previous — of the people who reached this stage, how many went on. This is the one that finds the broken step, because it is not dragged down by every loss above it.

Of the top — of everyone who entered, how many got this far. This is the one that goes in the report, because it is what the business earns.

Try "of the top only"

With only the from-the-top rate on, the invite step reads as an unremarkable slide from 93.1% to 32.5%, and the last step looks like the disaster. Switch back and the step rates say it plainly: the invite step keeps 34.9% and the paid step keeps 22.3% — two different problems, one of which was invisible until the denominator changed.
