A picture, and what is behind it

Press anywhere on the card and it turns about its middle to show the other side. Press again and it comes back. The button says the same thing in words, which is how anyone not using a pointer turns it — the card itself is not a button, because its back carries a link and a button inside a button is not markup.

Try tabbing through this page. The link on the back is unreachable until the card is showing it: a face turned away is taken out of the page rather than merely hidden from the eye.
