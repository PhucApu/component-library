Every column here is the same blue. Shading them darker where they are taller is the commonest way a bar chart goes wrong: it spends the only free channel the chart has on information the bar's own length already carries, and it makes the categories look ordered when they are not.

Why the scale starts at zero and stays there

A bar is read by its length, so a bar chart that starts at 200 makes 220 look like nothing and 410 look like double what it is. Columns and bars always include zero here; y-min can pull the floor further down but cannot lift it off zero. A line has no such obligation, because a line is read by its shape.

Long names go sideways

Press Horizontal bars. Category names that would have to be squeezed, thinned or turned on their side along the bottom get a whole line each down the left instead. The value rides the tip of each bar rather than sitting inside it, where a short bar would have clipped it.
