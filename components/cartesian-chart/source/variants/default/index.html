A chart drawn from a table

Point anywhere along the plot and a hairline finds the nearest month, then one read-out lists every series at that month. You never have to land on a 2px line: the thing being aimed at is a date.

The table below the chart is not a copy of the data — it is the data. Turn scripting off and what is left is every number, readable and complete. That is also why the table can be hidden but never removed: three of the eight light-theme series colours sit below the contrast rule on white, which is only allowed where the values are readable without colour as well.
