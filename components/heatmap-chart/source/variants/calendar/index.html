A quiet day and a day that has not happened

Look at the bottom-right corner. The last weekend has no squares at all — those days are still ahead. Every other Saturday and Sunday has a square in the quietest colour on the scale, because those days were counted and had nothing in them.

An empty cell is outside the range; a written 0 was measured. Collapsing the two is the fault that makes a calendar say "nobody worked" about a month nobody has reached yet — and it is invisible in the picture, which is exactly why it survives so long.
