What each one costs

Equal slices is the honest default: a colour means the same quantity wherever it appears, so two cells of one shade really are about the same size. It is the one to keep unless it has stopped saying anything.

Equal counts divides by rank instead — each step holds the same number of cells — so the quiet end spreads out and the picture comes back. The price is that the colour no longer means a fixed amount, and two cells of one shade may be ten apart or ten thousand. That is why it has to be asked for, and why the grid says so underneath itself whenever it is on.
