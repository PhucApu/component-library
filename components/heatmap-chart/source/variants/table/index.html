A heatmap is a table that has been coloured in

Of every chart in this collection this is the one where the table contract fits best, because the two are the same shape. Row headings become the row labels, column headings become the column labels, and each cell becomes a square. Nothing is configured in JavaScript and nothing is fetched.

Why the table matters more here than anywhere

On a bar chart the table is a second way to read a value. On a grid where every square is a colour and nothing else, it is the only exact one — a step of the scale is a range, not a number. Press Show the table: it is not a copy generated beside the grid, it is what the grid was drawn from.

The grid itself stays out of the accessibility tree entirely. Exposing both would read every figure twice — once as a field of squares and once as the table it came from — and the table is much the better of the two readings.

With no script

A complete, readable grid of numbers. For a page whose subject is a matrix of counts that is most of what anyone came for, and it is the same markup rather than a fallback written twice.
