Never colour alone

Every change carries three signals: an arrow, a word, and a colour — in that order of reliability. Two of the light-theme status colours sit below the contrast rule by design, and the arrow and the word are what make that legal. They are also what make the tile readable in a grey-scale print-out, or by the one reader in twelve who cannot separate the red from the green.
