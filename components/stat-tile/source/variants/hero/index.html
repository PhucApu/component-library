Exactly one per view

A hero figure works by being the only thing that size. Two of them is a tie, and a tie is the reader deciding what the page is about instead of the page saying so. The component cannot enforce this and does not pretend to — it is a rule for whoever lays out the page.

Same typeface, bigger

No display face and no serif. A different family here reads as decoration that wandered in from a marketing page rather than as emphasis, and it is the one place people reach for one.

The figures stay proportional. Equal-width digits are for columns that have to line up vertically; at this size they make a number like 1,284 look loose and badly spaced, because a 1 is being given the width of a 0.

Written short, on purpose

$1.28M is what the author wrote and 1284000 is what data-value carries. The component never abbreviates for you: it has no way to know whether your readers expect 1.28M, 1,28 Mio or 128 万, and guessing wrong is worse than not guessing.
