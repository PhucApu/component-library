A locked row is a wall, not a row you cannot pick up

Triage stays first and Closed stays last. Rows reorder freely on either side of them and never across. Treating a locked row as merely unpickable would let everything else slide underneath it and change its position anyway — which is the opposite of what "this one stays first" means.

Try to move In progress up: the move is refused and the refusal names what stopped it — "In progress cannot move past Triage". Running out of list at the very top says nothing at all, because nothing stopped you there.

Marked with a lock, not with a colour

A locked row carries no tint. In dark mode no tint cleared its contrast floor against its neighbour without going darker than the page behind it, and a fill nobody can see is worse than no fill. The lock sits where the handle would be, so the row is marked positively rather than by something being absent.
