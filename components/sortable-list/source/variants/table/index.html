A table costs two things, and both are worth saying

The handle goes inside the first cell, not beside it. A component cannot invent a table column: a new <td> would leave the header one heading short and every row misaligned from it.

Borders have to be separate. A row cannot carry a shadow while the table collapses its borders, and the shadow is what says "this one is off the page in your hand". Separate borders is the price of the lift.

Rows of different heights

Two of these rows carry a second line and are taller than the others. The distance a row travels to swap with its neighbour is the neighbour's height plus the gap, never its own — with one height any formula looks right, and with a real table the wrong one drops a place off from where you let go. Drag the short Starter row past the tall Team row and it commits where you expect, not at half its own height.
