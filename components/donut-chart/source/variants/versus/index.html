Which to use, plainly

Use the ring when the point is that these are parts of one whole and the split is lopsided — one dominant share, a couple of also-rans. The total in the hole is doing real work there.

Use bars when the point is the ordering, when the values are close, or when there are more than about four of them. Length is read accurately and angle is not, and no amount of design changes that.

The bars beside this ring are a few lines of CSS on this page, not another component — that is how little it costs to switch.
