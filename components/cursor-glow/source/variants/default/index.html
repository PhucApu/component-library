Move across it

The light sits exactly under the pointer — it never lags, because its position is taken from the last thing the pointer said rather than eased towards it. Only the brightness moves: it comes up as the pointer arrives and goes out where it left, rather than snapping off at the edge.

Nothing here is drawn per frame. The whole effect is one gradient whose centre is two custom properties, and those are written at most once a frame however fast the mouse reports.

The ink belongs to the surface, not to the page. This panel is dark whatever theme the page is in, so it names a warm light of its own and sets the glow to add light rather than lay a wash over it — a pale ink chosen for a pale page would be invisible here.

Bring a light with you

Move across this panel and the light comes with you.
