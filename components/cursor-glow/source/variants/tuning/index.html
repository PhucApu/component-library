Size and strength

--cursor-glow-size is the width of the light and --cursor-glow-strength is how much of it there is. A wide faint one reads as a room being lit; a small hard one reads as a torch.

Wide and faint

720px, at just over half strength.

Small and hard

140px, at full strength.

Colour, and what blending is for

Both panels below are dark and both carry the same warm ink. The second one adds its light to what is underneath instead of laying it over the top, which is what makes a glow look like light rather than like paint. It is left off by default because on a pale surface there is nothing to add light to.

normal, the default.

How long it takes to arrive

--cursor-glow-fade is the coming up and the going out. At 0ms the light is simply on wherever the pointer is; at 900ms it lingers after the pointer has left the region.
