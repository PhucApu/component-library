What was chosen, written out

Choosing an office opens a card over its pin: the name, the address, a link out to Google Maps, and a close button. The card is built here and anchored by whatever is drawing the map, which is why it is the same card over a real provider's tiles as over the drawing.

The link is maps/search/ rather than maps/dir/: the card offers to show you the place, which is a different question from how to get there. The directory below keeps the directions link, so both are on offer and neither is guessed at.

locator-select carries the name, the address and the region as well, so a page can put the office somewhere of its own instead. The component does not own that panel — it only says what happened.

Getting back out

A map that can only be zoomed in is a trap. reset() puts the whole country back and unmarks the office; the control on the map does the same, and so does the 0 key while the map has focus.
