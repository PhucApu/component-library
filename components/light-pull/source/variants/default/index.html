Pull it

Take hold of the cord anywhere along it and pull. It curves as it goes, follows your hand with a lag, and swings itself out after you let go — every joint is carrying its own momentum rather than playing a rehearsed swing, so a hard pull and a gentle one do not look the same.

Pull it far enough and let go: it works, the way a cord switch catches when it has been pulled past its stop. A short tug does nothing but set it swinging. Tab to the handle and press Space to do the same thing without a pointer.
